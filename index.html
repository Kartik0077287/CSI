THE PROBLEM

Connecting the three sides of the ecosystem

Students often struggle to understand the skills demanded by industry, while institutions lack real-time visibility into industry requirements. Companies, meanwhile, face difficulties finding appropriately skilled talent.

🎓

Students

Understand industry expectations, identify skill gaps and discover relevant internships and placement opportunities.

🏫

Academia

Map student skills, identify common skill gaps and align academic development with industry needs.

🏢

Industry

Communicate skill requirements, discover suitable talent and collaborate directly with institutions.
